In development

Get faster at your first clear.

Jungle Clear is a practice tool for League of Legends junglers, inspired by build-order trainers in other games. It compares each clear against a built-in set of fast, efficient reference clears for every champion, so you can see exactly where you lose time.

What it does

Everything is measured against reference clears that ship with the app, and the app only looks at your own clear.

Camp-by-camp splits

Detects each jungle camp you clear and shows your split time ahead of or behind the reference.

Casts and hits per camp

Counts your ability casts and basic attack hits in each camp and compares them to the reference clear.

Route card

Shows the camp order, which monster to hit first in each camp, and the planned ability use. It marks your progress as your own camps are cleared.

Reference clears per champion

Reference clears are bundled with the app and updated per patch, with more than one route option per champion where it makes sense.

Your own history

Your clears are recorded locally so you can track progress over time, per champion.

Progress window

A desktop window shows your history, best runs and trends for each champion, outside of the game.

Fair play

Jungle Clear is a training tool. It gives no live advice and no information about the other team.

What it does

  • Shows only your own data, compared to a pre-set reference
  • Lets you pick your own route before the game
  • Keeps your history on your own computer

What it never does

  • Track enemy cooldowns or summoner spells
  • Show ultimate timers or power-spike alerts
  • Tell you what to do during a game

The core features are free. The app uses game events provided through Overwolf and does not need a Riot API key.
